         A Butterfly That Changed the Future



 Eckels walked into the office of Time Travel. The big sign on
the wall  said, “NAME ANY YEAR IN THE PAST. WE TAKE YOU
THERE.” There was a TV on the  wall, and Mr. Keith, the new
president, was on the news.
 “How can we help you?” said Mr. Parker, the founder of Time
Travel.
  “I’ve always wanted to see a dinosaur.  Is  it possible?” asked
Eckels.
  “Sure,” said Mr. Parker. “We can take you back to the jungle
of 60 million years ago.”
  “Great,” said Eckels. “Can you take me there now?”
 “You’re  lucky.  There’s  a  time  machine  that  leaves  in  10
minutes,” said Mr. Parker.
 “This  is Travis, your travel guide,” he continued. “To stay safe,
you must follow his instructions.”




 Inside  the  time  machine,  there  were  two  other  travelers.
Eckels  sat down and  closed  his  eyes. He  felt  the machine
moving under his  feet. After only a few minutes, the machine
stopped.
 “We’ve  just  arrived  in  the  jungle  of 60  million  years  ago,”
Travis explained. “Do you see the path over there? You must
not leave  it at any time. Do not touch anything. You might  kill
something  by  accident.  A   little  change  here  can  cause
something unthinkable in the future.”
 Slowly, everyone got out of the time machine. The jungle was
covered in fog.  It was very quiet.
 “I’m so excited,” said Eckels.
 “Be  careful. Some of the dinosaurs are hungry,”  said  Travis.
“Just stay calm.  I don’t want you to do anything foolish.”
 “Don’t worry. I’m not a fool,” said Eckels.
 They all began to walk on the path.




 After some time, Travis whispered, “Look over there. There  is
a T. rex.”
 One hundred meters away, a T. rex slowly appeared.
  “It,” whispered Eckels.  “It  ...”
 “Sh! ”
  “It    ...  is huge.  I’ve  never seen  such a huge  thing.  I’m  so
scared,” said Eckels.
 “Sh   ...  quiet! ”  Travis said  angrily. “You don’t want  it to see
you.”
  “I can’t breathe,” said Eckels. “Get me out of here! ”
 “Don’t  panic,  Eckels,”  said  Travis.  “Turn  around,  and  walk
quietly back to the machine.”
 Eckels turned around and started to run. He was so  terrified
that he left the path by accident.
  “Eckels! ” shouted Travis. “Don’t leave the path! ”
 The  T. rex heard Travis and turned  its head toward him and
the other  travelers. There was a moment  of  silence. Then,  it
suddenly started to run toward them with a loud roar.
 “Run, everybody! Run! ” shouted Travis.
  Travis and the other travelers returned safely to the machine
and found Eckels inside.
 “You put  all  of us  in  danger,”  said  Travis  to  Eckels  in an
angry voice. “You left the path.”
 “I’m sorry, but  I didn’t  kill anything,” said Eckels. “There’s only
a  little mud on my shoes.”
  Travis  stared  at  Eckels  for  a  long  time  without  saying
anything.
  “Let’s go home,” said Travis finally.




 The machine stopped. They all returned to the present safely.
  “Is everything okay here?” asked Travis.
  “Fine. Welcome home! ” said Mr. Parker.
 Eckels looked around the room  carefully.  Everything looked
the same, except the big sign on the wall. The sign now said,
“NAIM ANY YEER EN THE PAST. WEE TAEK YU THAIR.” Eckels
was confused.
 Then, he saw the TV. The new president was on the news,
but   it was not Mr.  Keith. “What’s going on?” He got more
confused.
 He sat down and looked closely at the mud on his shoes.
 “No,  it  can’t  be,”  said  Eckels.  “It’s  all because  of  this  little
thing?” He was shocked.  In  the mud on  his  right  shoe, he
found a small dead butterfly.
