#H The Lipstick Effect
 The lipstick effect is an interesting economic phenomenon. When the economy is
bad, people often spend less money on expensive products. However, they still want
to enjoy small pleasures in their daily lives.
 For example, instead of buying an expensive designer bag, some people may buy a
new lipstick or a small perfume. These products are relatively inexpensive, but they
can make people feel happy.
 The lipstick effect shows that even during difficult economic times, people still look
for affordable ways to enjoy their lives.

#H The Halo Effect
 The halo effect is an interesting psychological phenomenon. It happens when one
good quality of a person or product influences how we think about their other
qualities.
 For example, imagine that you meet a student who is very good at English. You
may think that the student is also good at math and science, even though you do
not know their actual abilities.
 The halo effect can also be seen in advertising. When a famous celebrity promotes
a product, people may believe that the product is excellent because they like the
celebrity.
 The halo effect shows that our judgments are sometimes influenced by first
impressions rather than facts. Therefore, we should try to judge people and products
based on their actual qualities.
