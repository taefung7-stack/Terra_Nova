Animals in Cold Places
When it gets cold outside, people put on jackets and gloves. 
Some people drink hot chocolate. Others stay inside under 
blankets. But how about animals? How do they survive the 
cold? Let’s find out some of their survival secrets.
Polar bears
First, meet polar bears. Did you know that polar bears have 
clear fur? Their fur only looks white to us because of the 
sunlight. So, why is clear fur helpful in the cold? It lets more 
sunlight reach their skin. Here comes another interesting fact 
about polar bears. They have black skin! Dark skin helps them 
take in more heat. Having clear fur and black skin keeps polar 
bears warm.
Arctic foxes
Arctic foxes have dark skin and fur, too. They also use their 
big tails to keep themselves warm. When they sleep, they wrap 
their tails around their bodies like blankets! However, they may 
lose body heat through their ears in the cold air. Unlike other 
foxes in warm places, arctic foxes have small ears. Their ears 
are so small that they can help the foxes lose less heat.
Whales
Now, what about animals without fur? How do whales live in 
icy water? The winter sea is so cold that you cannot swim 
there, even for a second. However, whales have a special way 
to stay warm in icy water. They have thick fat under their skin. 
The fat can be more than 40 cm thick. It covers their bodies 
and keeps them warm. The fat also gives whales energy when 
they cannot find food for a long time.
Wood frogs
Lastly, in Alaska, winters can be too cold for wood frogs. 
What do they do to survive the freezing weather? These frogs 
freeze themselves! Up to 60 percent of their bodies freeze. 
They stop breathing, and their heart stops beating. But don’t 
worry. When spring comes, they come back to life. Nature is 
so amazing.
