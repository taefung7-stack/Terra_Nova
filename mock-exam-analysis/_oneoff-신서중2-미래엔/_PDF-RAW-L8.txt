Comparing Prices
Emily’s family is on vacation in Australia. They are traveling 
for a week. They are talking about their plans for the day.
Mom: Guys, what do you want to do today?
Nick: I really want to see koalas. They’re so cute!
Emily: There may be some koala tours. Should I ask for 
information at the desk?
Dad: You don’t have to. There are three tour brochures here: 
Smile Tour, Star Tour, and Top Tour. Let’s see which 
koala tour will be the best for us.
Smile Tour Star Tour Top Tour
$25/person for 1 hour
Choose the hours!
$60/person
3 hours
$110/pair
3 hours
Nick: First, let me compare the prices. The price of Smile Tour 
looks really good! It’s only $25.
Emily: Oh, it’s $25 for one hour. Look, the other tours are all 3 
hours.
Dad: Right. We need to find out the price for the same 
amount of time.
Nick: Okay, but how can we do that?
Emily: Let’s start with Smile Tour. If we take a 3-hour tour, it 
will cost $75.
Nick: Star Tour is $60 for 3 hours, and Top Tour is $110. Wow, 
now Star Tour is the cheapest!
Mom: Right, but we have one more thing to check.
Nick: What is it, Mom?
Mom: The price of Top Tour is $110, but it’s for two people.
Nick: Really? Where does it say that?
Mom: Here, it says $110 “per pair.” Do you know what a pair 
is? It means two.
Emily: Oh, now I know what to do! Divide 110 by 2 to get 
the price for one person.
Nick: It’s $55! Wow, it’s cheaper than Star Tour. That’s 
amazing! Let me make a chart.
Tour Smile Tour Star Tour Top Tour
Price for a 
3-hour tour/person
$75 $60 $55
Mom: Nick, you did a good job. Now you can see why Top 
Tour is the cheapest. Let’s go with this one.
