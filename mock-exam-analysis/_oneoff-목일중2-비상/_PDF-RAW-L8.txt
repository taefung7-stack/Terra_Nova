                  The Last Wild Race



 Do you know what the most challenging race in the world is?
It  is “The  Last Wild  Race”!  You should  join us  in Southern
Patagonia,  Chile.   It  is one  of  the  last  wild  areas on  Earth!
Every year, many teams from  all over the world join the race.
For this race, you need not only strength but also teamwork.


What Is the Race Like?
 The  race  course  is more  than 500  kilometers long and   it
changes every year. Do you know when the teams find out the
course  for the year?  Just a day before the race!  During the
race,  each team  goes  through  the  wild by  doing  different
activities. They  walk on rough  ground,  ride  bikes on tough
roads, and row boats on rapid water.
 The race  is not  just about  sports. The teams also need to
take care of nature. They must not make  fires or leave trash
behind  during  the  race. Everyone should  protect  the  natural
environment of Southern Patagonia.


What Are the Rules of the Race?
 To  join  the  event,  all teams must  follow a few  rules.  For
example, a team of four must have at least one man and one
woman. Every member must show that they are healthy. Also,
each team must pass some tests that check their skills in using
ropes,  reading maps, and  giving  first  aid.  This  is  important
because  the  race  is  very  tough, and everyone needs  to be
ready  for the challenges. To  finish the  race, each team must
reach every checkpoint. The first team to finish the race wins.
