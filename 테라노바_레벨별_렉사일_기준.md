## 3. 작성 가이드 (중요)

- **Lexile은 ① 문장당 평균 길이 + ② 어휘 빈도로 결정**된다. 글의 *총 길이*와는 무관.
- **길이를 늘릴 땐 문장을 길게 하지 말고, 짧은 문장 수를 늘린다** → Lexile 유지하며 분량만 확보.
- **MARS·VENUS는 TERRA 수준 길이(~110–120단어)로 작성** (저학년이라고 너무 짧지 않게).
- 영어 본문·선지·예문에 **한글 학년 표기 금지** → 영어로(중1=7th grade, 중2=8th grade, 중3=9th grade).
- 빌드 검증(`tools/validate-content.mjs`)의 `lexileRange` 권장값 = 위 1번 표의 밴드.

## 4. 동일 주제 예시 — 꿀벌(honeybees)·과학

같은 내용을 레벨별 목표 Lexile로 쓴 샘플. **레벨 간 난이도 단계 확인용.**

### MARS (초5) · ~140L
> Bees are small insects. They live in a hive. Many bees live there. A hive is their home. Bees fly to flowers. Flowers have sweet nectar. Bees drink the nectar. They take it home. Then bees make honey. Honey is sweet and yellow. People like to eat honey. Bees carry pollen too. Pollen sticks to their legs. They move it to other flowers. This helps flowers grow. Flowers grow into fruit. Bees work hard all day. They help plants grow. They help people get food. We need bees. We must keep bees safe.

### VENUS (초6) · ~260L
> Honeybees are small insects. They live together in a hive. One hive can hold many bees. Each bee has a job to do. Some bees clean the hive. Other bees look for food. They fly from flower to flower. They look for sweet nectar. They carry the nectar back home. Then they turn it into honey. The honey feeds the bees in winter. Bees also carry pollen on their legs. Pollen moves from flower to flower. This helps flowers make seeds and fruit. Farmers need bees for their crops. Without bees, many plants would not grow. That is why bees are so important. We should protect bees and their homes.

### TERRA (중1) · ~350L
> Honeybees are small insects that live together in a hive. One hive can hold thousands of bees, and each one has a job. A single queen bee lays all of the eggs. Worker bees fly from flower to flower to find nectar. They carry the sweet nectar back to the hive. There, the bees turn the nectar into honey. As they fly, pollen sticks to their bodies. The bees carry this pollen from one flower to another. This helps the flowers make seeds and fruit. Many farmers need bees to grow their crops. Sadly, the number of bees is falling in many countries. Without bees, much of our food would disappear.

### NEPTUNE (중2) · ~580L
> Honeybees are social insects that live together in colonies inside a hive. A single colony can hold up to fifty thousand bees, and each bee has a special job. Worker bees travel from flower to flower, collecting sweet nectar and storing it inside their bodies. When a bee finds good flowers, it returns and performs a special dance to tell the others where to go. Back at the hive, they slowly turn the nectar into honey, which feeds the colony during winter. While the bees move between flowers, pollen sticks to the tiny hairs on their legs. As they travel, they carry this pollen with them, a process called pollination. Pollination lets flowering plants produce seeds and fruit. Scientists say that bees help pollinate about a third of the food we eat. In recent years, however, bee numbers have dropped sharply, which worries farmers around the world.

### URANUS (중3) · ~715L
> Honeybees are highly social insects that live in large colonies, each containing tens of thousands of individuals with clearly divided roles. Worker bees may travel several kilometers in search of flowers, gathering nectar in a special stomach and carrying pollen on the hairs of their legs. When they return, the colony slowly turns the watery nectar into thick honey, a food source that keeps the hive alive through the cold months. Just as important is the pollen the bees move from flower to flower, a process known as pollination that allows plants to reproduce. Because bees pollinate roughly a third of the crops people eat, a sharp decline in their numbers could put the food supply at risk.
